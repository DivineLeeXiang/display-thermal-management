Why this Architecture Solves the Literature Bottleneck

Prior literature treated passive 2D spreaders and active micro-coolers as mutually exclusive. In reality:

1. Passive spreaders are interface-limited: Graphene loses up to 80% conductivity on SiO₂ due to Kapitza resistance. h-BN provides an ideal lattice match with zero electrical leakage into the subpixel drive lines.

2. Active coolers suffer from parasitic contact heating: By layering the micro-Peltier directly beneath an h-BN planar spreader, the Peltier cold-junction sees a pre-flattened baseline, boosting the active Coefficient of Performance (COP).
